Chinh sach quyen rieng tu

Cap nhat: 07/07/2026

Starship Escape ton trong quyen rieng tu cua nguoi choi. Chinh sach nay mo ta cach du lieu duoc xu ly trong phien ban hien tai.

Du lieu ca nhan

Phien ban hien tai khong yeu cau dang nhap, khong yeu cau ten, email, so dien thoai, vi tri chinh xac, danh ba, hinh anh ca nhan hoac thong tin thanh toan.

Du lieu luu tren thiet bi

Custom levels va cau hinh lien quan co the duoc luu trong localStorage cua trinh duyet hoac WebView. Du lieu nay nam tren thiet bi cua ban va co the bi xoa khi xoa du lieu trinh duyet, xoa cache hoac go ung dung.

Quang cao, phan tich va SDK ben thu ba

Phien ban hien tai khong tich hop quang cao, khong tich hop tracking analytics va khong dung SDK thu thap hanh vi nguoi dung. Neu cac dich vu nay duoc bo sung, chinh sach se duoc cap nhat truoc khi phat hanh.

Hosting va log ky thuat

Ung dung co the duoc phan phoi qua Firebase Hosting hoac GitHub Pages. Cac nha cung cap hosting co the xu ly log ky thuat co ban nhu dia chi IP, user agent va thoi gian truy cap theo chinh sach rieng cua ho.

Tre em

Tro choi co noi dung giai do pho thong. Truoc khi phat hanh tren Google Play, can hoan tat khai bao do tuoi, Data Safety va cac yeu cau Family Policy neu ung dung huong toi tre em.

Thay doi chinh sach

Khi bo sung dang nhap, Firebase cloud save, quang cao, phan tich, mua hang trong ung dung hoac tinh nang chia se cong khai, chinh sach nay can duoc cap nhat truoc ban phat hanh.
